Privacy Policy — Preelock

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Preelock is a local-first focus and productivity application developed by Prashant Dwivedi. Your privacy is central to how Preelock is built: the app does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Preelock has no user accounts, no cloud backend, no analytics SDKs, and no telemetry. All of your data — focus sessions, tasks, activity history, and settings — is stored locally on your own device in a local database and never leaves it.

Camera

Preelock offers an optional camera presence check that can verify you are at your desk during a focus session. When enabled, camera frames are processed entirely on your device for presence detection only. No images or video are recorded, saved, or transmitted anywhere.

Network Access

Preelock does not require an internet connection to function. The optional browser extension communicates only with the Preelock app running on your own device to enforce website-blocking rules during sessions.

Third Parties

Because no data leaves your device, no data is shared with any third party.

Children

Preelock does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
